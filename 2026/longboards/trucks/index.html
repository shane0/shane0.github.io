    trucks¶

    • I generally ride slalom trucks which turn tight therefore are narrow and loose with medium size wheel clearance
    • but they do not turn extremely tight like a surfskate which is less stable at high speed
    • when I was a little kid my first skateboard had bennett trucks I had no clue what they were
    • but I would get a fuzzy feeling when I leaned into tight turns
    • their turning profile is parabolic
    • back then I did not realize they are what slalom racers used and are legendary
    • now I have some fully upgraded vectors with spherical bearings from https://www.sk8kings.com/bennett-trucks.html
    • they are also used in ldp (long distance pump) boards

    kingpins¶

    AN6-22A: 3 AN6-23A: 3 AN6-24A: 7 AN6-25A: 4 AN6-26A: 2 AN6-27A: 1

    Total unique AN part numbers: 6

    The standard diameter for a skateboard or longboard kingpin is 3/8", which equals 6/16"—making all standard 3/8" longboard kingpins AN6 bolts (AN5 is 5/16", which is used for wheel axles, not kingpins).

    The updated AN6 bolt sizes and sources:

    Randal R2 Kingpin: AN6-25A (2 3/4") or AN6-27A (2 7/8") Kingpin Type: Reverse Kingpin (RKP) RipTide Bushing Height: 0.60" Standard Longboard

    Paris V3 Kingpin: AN6-24A or AN6-25A Kingpin Type: Reverse Kingpin (RKP) RipTide Bushing Height: 0.60" Boardside / 0.50" Roadside

    Paris Savant Kingpin: AN6-24A or AN6-25A Kingpin Type: Precision Reverse Kingpin (RKP) RipTide Bushing Height: 0.60" Standard Longboard

    Dont Trip Poppy Kingpin: AN6-24A (2 9/16") Kingpin Type: Precision RKP with Spherical Pivot RipTide Bushing Height: 0.60" Standard Longboard

    Dont Trip Slalocybin Kingpin: AN6-23A (2 1/2") or AN6-26A (2 13/16") Kingpin Type: Precision Slalom RKP RipTide Bushing Height: 0.75" Tall Longboard

    Synergy Chorus Rear Kingpin: AN6-22A (2 5/16") or AN6-23A Kingpin Type: Precision Adjustable LDP Inverted Kingpin RipTide Bushing Height: 0.75" Tall or 0.60" Standard depending on setup

    Zealous V2 Kingpin: AN6-24A Kingpin Type: Precision Slalom / Downhill RKP RipTide Bushing Height: 0.75" Tall Boardside / 0.50" Street Roadside

    Valkyrie Mk4 Kingpin: AN6-24A or AN6-25A Kingpin Type: Precision RKP RipTide Bushing Height: 0.75" Tall Longboard

    Bennett Vector Kingpin: AN6-23A (2 1/2") Kingpin Type: Traditional Kingpin (TKP) High Rake RipTide Bushing Height: 0.75" Tall Boardside / 0.50" Street Roadside

    Sources & Sizing Reference:

    • Sk8Kings Aircraft Bolt Sizing Matrix (cross-references AN6 bolt lengths to Bennett, Don't Trip, Randal, and Tracker setups).
    • Pavedwave / LDPenthusiast Hardware Guides (AN diameter standard: AN3 = 3/16", AN4 = 1/4", AN5 = 5/16", AN6 = 3/8"; dash numbers represent grip length in 1/8" increments, "A" denotes undrilled shank).
