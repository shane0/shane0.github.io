## surfskate

surfskate boards you can say are low geared and turn extremely sharp similar to a surfing you can "pump" it to accelerate from a stand still

on most of my surfskates you can tune the angle of the rear truck which holds the wheels on the board so the gearing changes and it feels like a shorter or longer surfboard

when you ride a surfskate downhill you have to keep the speed down so you turn back and forth aka switchback carving

I recently built a downhill slalom racing board I will add a video of that later

## my longboard stories

fun fact - I'm 53 and I started skateboarding (again)

I am trying to keep it safe for example drive to the burke gilman and use a long distance pumper the biggest danger there are the morons in spandex or the mob flying around on ebike motorcycles or onewheels I have nothing against ebikes etc. but if you are going over the trail limit which is 15mph you need to go ride on the street with the cars not the grandmas with baby strollers

that said my downhill slalom board is so stable I find my self going increasingly fast and loving it lol

I started skateboarding in elementary school my mom got me I believe a variflex with bennett trucks

I used to get an exhilarating feeling carving on it - carving is leaning hard into curves

now I have a custom modified slalom racing bennett truck and learned why it used to be so exhilarating

they have a design that causes a high rate "divey" turning profile

I skated everywhere!  

I bicycled before that and I cannot remember what happened to my bmx bike

I either lost it or it broke but it never occurred to me to ask for another bike I just switched to skateboarding

later I upgraded it to a powell lance mountain which is a high value collector now I should have kept mine lol

I don't know what happened to that board I think I traded it for a per welinder then a skull freestyle deck and probably gave that away or lost it in a move

anyway I am not sure why I ever quit skating I did not get bored with it I wish I'd stuck with it

one reason I took a break is I broke my wrist bones and had a compound fracture but that was being dump I tried riding vert ramps with no training lol
