- earth evolved the human 300,000 years ago
- we started powering farms using animals for 5000 years
- 150 years ago we powered farms using oil
  - oil power allowed our species to enter ecological overshoot in 1970 at 3 billion
  - oil powered farms over consume natural resources and over pollute
  - this also causes dead zones in the ocean
- estimates are that due to pollution and consumption the earth can now sustain only 2 billion humans
- <https://www.santafe.edu/research/projects/cities-scaling-sustainability>
- focusing your time on wants not needs can unintentionally destroy your needs
